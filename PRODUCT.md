# Product

## Register

product

> Primary surface is the officer-facing application (dashboard, verification, audit).
> A single marketing-style landing page (brand register) sits in front of the app login.

## Users

- **Procurement Officer (primary):** evaluates bids at a CPSE such as CPCL. Needs a fast, trustworthy per-bidder verdict with evidence, side-by-side comparison, and a defensible audit trail. Works at a desk, on a wide monitor, under time pressure and audit scrutiny.
- **Verification Analyst:** prepares compliance for the officer; bulk uploads, re-runs checks, resolves unverifiable items.
- **Audit / Vigilance Officer:** post-facto scrutiny; needs an immutable, provenance-stamped record.
- **Admin / IT:** configures providers, tender templates, roles, and live/mock toggles.
- **Evaluators / judges (SIH context):** must grasp the value in under a minute and trust that the verdicts are explainable and real.

## Product Purpose

PRAMAAN verifies bidder eligibility and statutory compliance for Government e-Marketplace (GeM) procurement. It ingests bidder documents and tender rules, cross-verifies claims against government sources (real where accessible, faithfully simulated where gated), runs an explainable compliance engine, detects forgery and cross-portal fraud and cartels, and produces a compliance score, risk level, AI recommendation, and an auditable report. The officer always makes the final decision; the platform is advisory decision support. Success = days of manual multi-portal checking compressed into minutes, with every verdict traceable to evidence.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, precise, trustworthy, and modern. Voice is calm and factual, never salesy or alarmist. Three words: **authoritative, transparent, efficient**. It should feel like official government infrastructure that a career officer would trust, yet contemporary enough that it does not look like a dated portal. Adverse findings are phrased for the officer's consideration, never as accusations.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS: identical icon+heading+text card grids, uniform spacing with no hierarchy, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, hero-metric templates, gradient text, glassmorphism-as-default, over-rounded (24px+) cards, ghost cards (1px border + wide soft shadow).
- Dated government portal clichés: cluttered link dumps, harsh table borders, clip-art, marquee tickers, low-contrast gray-on-tint text.
- Crypto/fintech neon dark dashboards; playful consumer app styling. This is public procurement, not a startup landing.

## Design Principles

1. **Verdict first, evidence one click away.** Lead with the answer (score, band, top reasons); make the officer never assemble the conclusion themselves.
2. **Every number traces to evidence.** No opaque figures. Provenance (source, method, timestamp, mode) is visible on data, not hidden.
3. **Officer in control.** The UI never auto-disqualifies; AI output is labelled advisory and the human decision is a distinct, deliberate action.
4. **Honest about data.** LIVE / SIMULATED / SNAPSHOT is always badged. Trust is earned by transparency, not by faking "live".
5. **Institutional calm.** Restrained palette, strong hierarchy, purposeful density. Impressive through clarity and craft, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA (GIGW 3.0 / UX4G aligned). Every status conveyed by colour AND icon AND text, never colour alone. Full keyboard operability, visible focus rings, labelled forms with inline errors, responsive to 200% zoom and 320px width, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, dark and high-contrast themes supported. No Ashoka State Emblem (2005 Act); neutral wordmark with a clear "not an official Government of India site" disclaimer.
